RE6 Audio Editing
Work in Progress / 未完结
============================================================

中文
====

一、当前状态

RE6 SPC 的解包、读取、单槽替换、重建和回读已经有验证过的底层能力，但“自动把任意
替换音频调成游戏内正常听感”的编辑工具尚未完成。本目录不提供通用一键音频 MOD 工具。

二、不同 SPC 不能共用一套参数

不同 SPC 的差异目前可能来自流编码参数、SPC 元数据、目标槽位、游戏运行时播放规则，
或这些因素的组合。现在还不能把它们全部归因于同一种“编码”，也不能把一个成功参数
复制给所有 SPC。

已观察到的 Em4700 例子：

1. `snd_em4700_10.spc` 的已测试目标流为零基索引 22。当前候选补偿为：
   `db M +11.5 dB`、音高 `+45`、速度 `1.5x`，且不把补尾静音或强制对齐原时长当作
   默认规则。这个候选来自游戏内试听：只做音高补偿、速度保持 1.0 时仍会有变慢听感。

2. `snd_em4700_body.spc` 的已测试目标流同样是零基索引 22，但它不应继承 `_10` 的
   `+45` 音高规则。给 BODY 加上 `+45` 后，游戏里会直接听到明显升高的音调，因此 BODY
   应从音高 `0` 开始，再用实际游戏试听决定是否需要音量、速度或其他调整。

结论：`_10` 和 `body` 至少代表两种不同的处理情况。是否属于不同编码、不同运行时
处理或不同资源语义，仍需继续取证。

三、让 Em4700 在游戏内听起来正常，而不是尖嗓子的原则

目标不是把某个数字固定套上去，而是让游戏内播放后的音高、速度、响度和时长接近目标
原声的实际听感。

1. 先提取未改动的目标 SLOT，并记录其原始采样率、声道、时长、编码载荷和游戏内听感。
2. 对替换素材单独做音量、音高和速度候选；不要把 `_10` 的 `+45 / 1.5x` 套给 BODY。
3. 在游戏中 A/B 对比。若声音变尖、变慢、变低或拖尾，当前候选就是失败，不可因为
   “文件能回包”而继续使用。
4. 记录每个 SPC、每个零基 SLOT 的最终参数和游戏验证结果。听感参数是按槽位保存的
   候选，不是全局预设。

四、未来 AI 自动处理应当做什么

真正有价值的 AI 音频工具不应只是包装一堆 FFmpeg 按钮。它至少需要：

1. 自动扫描 ARC/DLC ARC 的所有音频提供者，并定位实际 SPC 与 SLOT。
2. 解码原始流，保留每个 SLOT 的零基索引、边界、哈希、时长、采样率、声道、编码与
   已验证参数。
3. 把多个 SLOT 可视化为带明确边界的总时间轴，但不能靠静音重新猜切片边界。
4. 分析替换音频和游戏录音的波形、频谱、速度、音高和响度差异。
5. 结合动作、场景和声音用途匹配候选 SLOT，并给出置信度；置信度不足时必须让人类确认。
6. 为每个 SLOT 单独生成音量、音高、速度、时长和编码候选，重建 SPC 后再做游戏内
   A/B 验证与下一轮参数调整。

五、为什么现在不做完整音频工具

如果只有普通编辑工具，人类仍要自己试听几十个短音频、判断用途、选择 SLOT、切片、
调 dB/音高/速度、进游戏触发动作并记住每一轮结果。这样只是把“人类自己编辑音频”
换成“人类自己点更多按钮”，依旧麻烦、费力和耗时。

在 AI 还不能稳定理解声音用途、游戏上下文和最终听感之前，过早制作大界面不会真正
减轻工作量。当前更合理的做法是保留已验证的 SPC 编码/重建能力，持续记录成功与失败
参数，等待 AI 能把语义识别、候选匹配、游戏录音对比和调参迭代真正自动化后再开发。

六、Game Over 声音切断：失败结论

此前的“Game Over 声音切断”方向没有完成。最后的资源测试只让一组 BGM 候选静音，
并没有证明真正的 Game Over 声音被单独定位或切断。

当前结论：游戏的 SOUND 切断机制仍未理解。不能把“BGM 静音”称为“成功删除目标声音”，
不能作为正式补丁，也不能当作已完成的音频功能。后续若继续，必须从原版资源链、实际
触发顺序和单一资源验证重新开始。

English
-------

1. Current Status

Verified low-level abilities exist for RE6 SPC extraction, reading, single-slot
replacement, rebuilding, and read-back. An editor that automatically makes an
arbitrary replacement sound correct in game is not finished. This directory does
not provide a universal one-click audio MOD tool.

2. Different SPC Files Cannot Share One Parameter Set

Observed differences can come from stream encoding parameters, SPC metadata,
target slot, runtime playback behavior, or a combination of these. They cannot
yet all be called one kind of "encoding," and a successful parameter set cannot
be copied to every SPC.

Observed Em4700 examples:

1. The tested target stream in `snd_em4700_10.spc` is zero-based index 22. Its
   current candidate compensation is `db M +11.5 dB`, pitch `+45`, and speed
   `1.5x`. Padding with silence or forcing original duration is not the default.
   This candidate came from in-game listening: pitch compensation alone with
   speed 1.0 still sounded slow.

2. The tested target stream in `snd_em4700_body.spc` is also zero-based index
   22, but it must not inherit the `_10` pitch `+45` rule. Applying `+45` to
   BODY produces an obviously raised in-game pitch. BODY should begin at pitch
   `0`, then use real game listening to determine gain, speed, or other edits.

Conclusion: `_10` and `body` represent at least two different processing cases.
Whether the reason is different encoding, different runtime handling, or
different resource semantics still requires evidence.

3. Making Em4700 Sound Normal in Game Instead of Squeaky

The target is not to impose one number. It is to make post-playback pitch,
speed, loudness, and duration resemble the intended in-game reference.

1. Extract the untouched target SLOT and record sample rate, channels, duration,
   encoded payload, and in-game listening result.
2. Make gain, pitch, and speed candidates for the replacement independently;
   never apply `_10`'s `+45 / 1.5x` to BODY by default.
3. Compare in game. A squeaky, slow, low, or trailing sound means the candidate
   failed, even if the SPC rebuilt without error.
4. Save final parameters and game verification per SPC and per zero-based SLOT.
   Listening parameters are slot-specific candidates, not global presets.

4. What Future AI Automation Must Do

A useful AI audio tool must do more than expose FFmpeg buttons. It needs to
scan ARC/DLC providers, preserve every SLOT boundary and metadata, visualize a
timeline without guessing cuts from silence, compare source and in-game audio,
match likely slots with confidence, generate per-slot candidates, rebuild SPC,
and iterate from in-game A/B recordings. Low confidence must require human
confirmation.

5. Why a Full Audio Tool Is Not Being Built Yet

With only a conventional editor, a person still has to listen to many short
clips, identify their purpose, choose slots, cut audio, tune dB/pitch/speed,
trigger actions in game, and track every result. That merely turns manual audio
editing into manual use of more buttons: it remains tedious, labor-intensive,
and slow.

Until AI can reliably understand sound purpose, game context, and listening
targets, a large UI will not remove the real workload. The sensible current
path is to retain verified SPC encoding/rebuild capability, document successful
and failed parameters, and wait to build a full editor until semantic matching,
in-game recording comparison, and iteration can actually be automated.

6. Game Over Sound Cut: Failure Record

The previous Game Over sound-cut direction is not complete. Its final resource
test only silenced a group of BGM candidates. It did not prove that the actual
Game Over sound was individually located or cut.

Current conclusion: the game's SOUND cut mechanism is still not understood.
"BGM muted" must not be called a successful target-sound removal, cannot be a
release patch, and is not a completed audio feature. Any continuation must
restart from original resource chains, true trigger order, and single-resource
validation.
