AI Boundaries and Personal RE6 MOD Notes / AI 的边界与个人 RE6 MOD 记录
==========================================================================

中文
====

一、开篇：AI 目前的边界

AI 现在还不能稳定、完整地理解语音、音效、三维内容、三维软件界面和人的全部操作
流程。它可以检索资料、阅读文件、整理步骤、提出假设和协助试错，但不能把一次回答
当成已经验证过的 3D MOD 结论。

本文只是一次简略而系统的个人 RE6 模型 MOD 记录，不是专业教学，也不是适用于所有
模型、所有游戏或所有软件版本的黄金标准。参数、骨骼、权重、材质和资源合同都可能因
模型而变；最终仍要回读文件并进游戏验证。

二、获取模型：先找现有工具，再考虑逆向

模型可能来自手机的岛屿 AI APP、模之屋等允许使用的模型社区、自己拥有并获准处理的
其他游戏资源，或已知格式的解包结果，例如 VPK、PKG。手机应用导出的模型有时会在
设备内部存储的 Android 目录或应用自己的导出目录中；实际位置会随应用和 Android
版本变化，应先在设备中搜索最近下载或导出的文件。

处理其他游戏资源时，必须只使用自己拥有、获许可或可合法研究的内容，不应非法分发
受保护的原始资源。

核心方法是先让 AI 搜索互联网和已有工具。成熟工具往往能把一整晚的格式推理或逆向
缩短成一个可复现的工作流。AI 当然可能推导出解码或逆向方法，但在已有可靠工具时，
先使用工具是合理的捷径；后续再根据需要理解文件结构和验证结果。

三、Blender、3ds Max 与模型准备

1. 先把源模型导入 Blender。若来源是 MMD，MMD -> Blender 或 Blender -> MMD 的
   具体转换步骤目前没有重新验证，也记不完整。可参考
   https://www.nexusmods.com/residentevil6/mods/692 ，以及以前的 AI 日志，不能把
   本文当作 MMD 转换说明。
2. 把 Blender 的 Undo History 设得尽可能高，便于在姿势、权重或导入操作出错后回退。
3. 把模型摆成接近 RE6 尺寸的 T Pose。之后可尝试权重转移，或在 3ds Max 中使用
   Skin Wrap 等方法继续绑定。
4. 个人流程中常常只导出 Mesh。骨骼一起导入或导出到 3ds Max 时，视图可能会显得很
   混乱；这不是骨骼不重要，而是要按实际目标决定是否保留和处理它。
5. 做 MOD 时，自定义 Mesh 应替换明确的原版 Mesh，并使用对应原版 Mesh 的名字。若
   不想把所有内容合成一个大 Mesh，可以选择多个不同的原版游戏 Mesh：分别把自己的
   Mesh 重命名为相应原版 Mesh 名，再按这些目标做绑定和权重处理。
6. 在 EM4700 的游戏内观察中，LOD 2 分组可能与阴影有关。若自定义 Mesh 没有 LOD 2，
   它的 Mesh 名应与原 LOD 2 Mesh 名区分开；模型可能没有阴影，但这通常不是最重要的
   问题。以上仅在 EM4700 敌人资源上做过游戏内验证，玩家角色尚未验证，不能直接外推。
7. 权重绑定、刷权重和参数调整通常是最费时、最需要经验的部分。这里交给各位 MOD
   作者各自处理：本文不提供所谓万能参数，也不声称某一组数值能适用于换过的模型。
8. 贴图烘焙可同样参考上述 Nexus 页面。此前 EM4700 的工作记录里也写过贴图处理，
   但应把它视为一个样本，而不是所有角色的固定流程。

三点一、从随附 AI LOG 抓取的具体操作流程

以下流程整理自随附 AI LOG 中的 `Reference Workflow`。它是原记录作者的工作样本，
不是独立复现后的通用规范；尤其是 DDS 格式，必须和当前目标 RE6 TEX 的实际合同及
游戏内结果一起确认。

模型、骨骼与 Mesh 分组：

1. 对 MMD 模型，参考文本给出的起点是先在 Blender 导入 PMX，再缩放模型，并对照 RE
   游戏原骨骼检查整体对齐。腿和脚的对齐可能需要单独处理；参考文本明确表示部分效果仍
   未解决，因此不能假装这是已经完全自动化的步骤。
2. 在 Blender 修正比例、姿势、FBX 材质或丢失贴图问题后，再将 FBX 交给 3ds Max。
   Mesh 分割可在 Blender 或 Max 完成；随后才是 Skin Wrap、权重转移、重命名和导出。
3. 先找出要替换的原版 Mesh 名，再让自定义 Mesh 使用该名字。参考文本的经验是，大腿或
   腿部对应的原版 Mesh 名常是关键；它不是所有模型都可照抄的命名规则。
4. EM4700 的参考样本要求把自定义模型拆为头、上半身、腿三个 Mesh。在 LOD 1 中，
   参考文本记录的对应槽位是 Head Mesh 62、Legs Mesh 37、Upper Body Mesh 15。这是
   EM4700 的样本，不是角色或其他敌人的固定编号。
5. 参考文本把 Mesh 7 记为“没有顶点、但带 Skin modifier”的 Mesh，并建议跳过；也曾
   因旧导出器问题跳过 Mesh 61 的牙齿 Skin Wrap。后者是历史限制，当前导出器是否仍
   有同样问题必须重新测试，不能直接当成规则。
6. 先完成一套 LOD Mesh，再克隆 Instance 供其他 LOD 使用，不要把每个 LOD 的每个
   Mesh 都独立重做。参考文本推测 LOD 2 分组负责游戏阴影；本文件也只把它当作 EM4700
   上的观察，不外推到角色或其他资源。
7. 若使用 `RE6 COPY INSTANCE.ms`，它可以批量生成 LOD 的克隆 Instance，但参考文本说明
   它没有自动识别能力，Mesh 条目仍需人工逐个加入、处理和清理。

UV2 重排与检查：

为什么 RE6 脚本需要导出 UV2：我不会熟练地排列 UV1，因此个人流程是把模型导入
3ds Max 后，对 UV2 使用 Pack & Normalize，让 UV2 承载完整展开、无重叠的贴图布局，
而不去碰原来的 UV1。为使这一布局能被后续 RE6 导出流程保留和使用，我才要求脚本
支持导出 UV2。其他 MOD 作者可能更熟练，能以更好的方式整理 UV；这是个人工作流的
绕行办法，不应被误写为所有人的最佳做法。

1. 参考文本建议把 3DS 作为并排参考，因为它的导入/导出状态最接近原生 .MOD。
2. 对所有准备导出的 Mesh 添加 Unwrap UVW modifier，并切换到 Channel 2。
3. 使用 Move 和 Pack & Normalize，让 UV 岛完全摊开且没有重叠。
4. 将 Unwrap UVW 拖到 Skin 和 Skin Wrap modifier 下方，再使用 Collapse To，把新的
   UV2 布局固化到 Editable Mesh。
5. 可把同一层的 Mesh 暂时用 Attach 合并做统一检查；检查结束后恢复合并前状态。

贴图烘焙：

1. 按 `0` 打开 Render to Texture，选择 `Using Existing Channel = 2`。
2. 在 Output 添加 DiffuseMap，通过三点按钮将输出格式设为 PNG。
3. Color Management 选择 `Color Space Conversion`，色彩空间设为 sRGB；参考文本警告
   `No Conversion` 会得到发灰、低饱和的贴图。
4. 将 Target Map Slot 设为 Base Color，Element Background 设为纯白，分辨率设为
   4096 x 4096；启用 Baked Material 的 `Render to Files Only` 后开始烘焙。输出图可
   作为后续 Base、Normal、Metallic 贴图制作的基础素材，不表示它们已经自动正确生成。
5. 参考文本记录的旧 DDS 提示是：关闭 MIPMAP；BM 和 NM 使用 DXT5 Interpolated Alpha，
   黑白 MM 使用 DXT1 1-bit Alpha。这与本文件第四节当前个人设置（BM/MM 为 DXT1
   No Alpha，NM 为 DXT5 Interpolated Alpha）不一致。两者不能盲目混用：应以目标
   TEX 版本、原贴图合同和游戏内回读结果决定最终格式。

相邻 Mesh 的权重缝合：

RE6 脚本提供不同 Mesh 之间的权重缝合功能，但目前效果不理想，仍需要后续研究和实测。

四、当前玩家角色贴图设置：仅为已使用过的个人参数

我目前按教学和实际测试，为玩家角色使用以下设置：

    尺寸：4096 x 4096
    BM：DXT1，No Alpha
    MM：DXT1，No Alpha
    NM：DXT5 Interpolated Alpha
    MIPMAP：全部不生成

这些不是 RE6 的通用规范，也不是每个 MOD 都必须照抄的答案。我的做法是把所有 UV
岛集中到一张贴图里；官方资源常把头和身体分到不同贴图。统一的大贴图放回 ARC、又不
改变贴图路径时，容量会非常大，单个 ARC 甚至可能膨胀到数百 GB 而无法接受。

因此我让 AI 在 MRL 中把贴图重定向到游戏目录 nativePC 下自定义的松散文件路径。MRL
内必须从 nativePC 后的第一个目录开始写：

    正确：1\2\3
    错误：\1\2\3

游戏会按下面的形式寻找文件：

    nativePC\1\2\3

也就是说，路径不能以反斜杠开头，也不要在 MRL 字符串里写 nativePC 前缀。更完整的
规则和例子见同一发布目录的 `AI MRL PROJECT\01_MRL_Texture_Path_Redirection_CN_EN.txt`。

五、缺失资源与测试开关

启用 Ultimate Trainer 的 Missing Files Fix 后，删除资源不一定会让游戏立即闪退，因此
可以继续观察资源链和路径问题。这是测试辅助，不表示删除资源后的 MOD 已经可以稳定
发布。

目前没有测试环境来确认 Wilsonso's shader pack 是否能在 ARC 内 TEX 被删除时，始终
避免 Missing File 弹窗或崩溃。这是一个未完成问题：请自行测试并记录结果；不能把它
当作已验证结论。

六、EM4700 / EM4701 的贴图、手部与 UV 观察

敌人资源通常集中在相关 ARC 组内。这里“没有单独做手部 MOD”不等于手部不需要 MOD；
它只是为了省去单独制作和绑定手部而采用的工作流选择，仍必须按实际资源链确认。

在当前 EM4700 观察中，游戏手部读取的是 EM4700 自己的 UV，而不是 EM4701 的模式。
已观察到 EM4700 和 EM4701 的身体贴图实际使用 EM4700 那一组：修改 EM4701 内部对应
贴图，在该测试中没有生效。一个尚未完成验证的推测是，EM4701 的 .MOD 实际也可能读取
EM4700 的 UV；需要继续用二进制回读和游戏内单变量测试确认，不能当作定论。

因此，如果 EM4701 会在游戏中单独出现，EM4700 与 EM4701 的 UV 岛需要兼容，最好按
同一布局制作；否则 EM4701 上半身可能出现贴图错乱。

玩家角色的手部也有独立的 HAND ARC。在身体文件中可以看到，玩家角色 .MOD 的多数
手部 Mesh 位于 LOD 3。当前测试中，把这些 Mesh 的 LOD 字段设为 0 后，角色在游戏中
会失去手部。手部 UV 与 HAND ARC 的 UV 似乎会遵循被修改 Mesh 的 UV：当修改的 Mesh
本身带有双手时，额外不做手部权重绑定，游戏内也可能看起来正常。原因尚未研究清楚；
这是学习时发现许多作者也在使用的省工路线，不是已经解释完成的资源合同。

对某些玩家资源，在 Missing Files Fix 开启时似乎可以省掉部分贴图，但这只能作为临时
测试手段，不能当作稳定发布基线。之后若完成其他敌人、武器、玩家角色、场景或物件的
测试，应继续以“资源实际由谁读取”为准记录，而不是只按文件名猜测。

七、身体、手和头部的生产方式

常见做法是先完成一个带手的身体，再把头部绑定上去，在 3ds Max 中用权重工具和刷子
平滑权重。完成并备份后，身体 MOD 中的原头部 Mesh 可以删除，生成新的 MOD；但如果
需要表情，通常仍要回到头部 MOD 或头部相关资源处理。

八、跨 ARC 替换 MOD / MRL：当前只是待验证的假设

在 Wilsonso's shader pack 存在，或者 Ultimate Trainer 同时开启 Missing Files Fix 与
Render Everything 时，某些场景 ARC 和角色 ARC 之间也许可以直接放入准备好的 .MOD 和
.MRL，以较快方式复用自己的模型，同时承接目标对象的动作、声音和台词。

这不是已经验证的通用做法。贴图是否同路径、不同对象的动画容器、骨骼数量、BMT、碰撞
和初始化资源都会造成限制，目前还没有整理出适用于全部角色和敌人的规则。

uSm5919 是反例：曾把 uEm4700 的 MOD、MRL 和 TEX 放入 uSm5919 容器，删除不兼容的
BMT 后，完整 Mesh 可以显示；但 uEm4700 有 83 根骨骼，uSm5919 原生动画容器只有 27
根骨骼，动画中仍会闪烁或消失。这只能证明显示资源被装入，不能证明角色替换稳定。
正确方向仍是以目标原生骨骼为模板，在 3D 软件中重新绑定并检查 Skin 权重。

九、为什么留下这些记录

如果未来 AI 能真正理解 3D 图形、三维软件和完整 RE6 MOD 工作流，人们或许只需提供
创意和目标效果，而不必独自掌握所有技术步骤。更远的未来，在版权期限届满且具备合法
权利的前提下，对游戏资源的充分理解也许能启发用现代引擎做出面向下一代玩家的全新
演绎；这只是愿景，不是现在的能力，也不是对任何受保护资源的授权。

十、最后说明

本文不是合格的完整 MOD 教学。我目前没有足够时间、环境或专业知识去覆盖所有模型、
权重、材质、动画和游戏资源情况。做 MOD 时应当多和 AI 一起思考、保留备份、做回读和
游戏内验证；更要尊重其他人的权益，不侵犯他人的利益，把重点放在技术研究和自己的
创作上。


English
-------

1. Opening: the current boundary of AI

AI does not yet reliably and completely understand voice, sound design, 3D
content, 3D software interfaces, or every human workflow. It can search for
references, inspect files, organize steps, form hypotheses, and help with
experiments, but one answer is not a verified 3D-MOD conclusion by itself.

This is a compact personal record of an RE6 model-MOD workflow, not a
professional tutorial or a gold standard for every model, game, or software
version. Parameters, skeletons, weights, materials, and resource contracts can
change from model to model. Always read the output back and verify it in game.

2. Getting models: find established tools before reverse engineering

Models may come from a mobile Island AI App, permitted model communities such
as MoZhiWu, game resources that you own and are allowed to process, or unpacked
known formats such as VPK and PKG. A mobile application may save exports under
an Android directory in internal storage or in its own export folder. The exact
location varies by application and Android version, so search the device for
recently downloaded or exported files first.

When handling other-game resources, use only content you own, have permission
to use, or can lawfully research. Do not unlawfully redistribute protected
original assets.

The core method is to ask AI to search the web and existing tooling first. A
mature tool can turn a night of format reasoning or reverse engineering into a
repeatable short workflow. AI may be able to derive a decoder or a reversing
method, but using proven tools first is a valid shortcut when they already
exist. Understand the format and verify the result when the work requires it.

3. Blender, 3ds Max, and model preparation

1. Import the source model into Blender. The precise MMD -> Blender or Blender
   -> MMD route has not been re-verified and is no longer fully remembered.
   See https://www.nexusmods.com/residentevil6/mods/692 and prior AI logs;
   this document is not an MMD conversion guide.
2. Raise Blender's Undo History as far as practical so pose, weighting, or
   import mistakes can be rolled back.
3. Arrange an approximate RE6-scale T-pose. Then try weight transfer, or use
   methods such as Skin Wrap in 3ds Max for further binding.
4. The personal workflow often exports only meshes. Skeletons can make the 3ds
   Max view look confusing when imported or exported together. That does not
   make skeletons unimportant; retain and process them when the target needs it.
5. For a MOD, each custom mesh should replace a specific native mesh and use
   that native mesh's name. If one large mesh is not desired, choose several
   different native game meshes, rename the custom meshes to the corresponding
   native mesh names, and bind and weight them against those targets.
6. In the EM4700 in-game observation, the LOD 2 group may be related to the
   shadow mesh. If a custom mesh has no LOD 2, its mesh name should be distinct
   from the original LOD 2 mesh name. The model may then have no shadow, but
   that is usually not the most important problem. This has been verified in
   game only for the EM4700 enemy resource. It has not been verified for player
   characters and must not be generalized to them.
7. Skin binding, weight painting, and parameter adjustment are normally the
   most labor-intensive and experience-dependent parts. Each modder should
   handle them for the target model. No values in this note are universal.
8. Texture baking can also refer to the Nexus page above. Existing EM4700 notes
   contain texture-processing examples, but they are examples, not a fixed
   workflow for every character.

3.1 Concrete workflow captured from the attached AI log

The following workflow was distilled from the `Reference Workflow` in the
attached AI log. It is the original record author's working sample, not an
independently reproduced universal rule. This matters especially for DDS
settings: confirm the actual RE6 TEX contract of the target and the in-game
result.

Model, skeleton, and mesh grouping:

1. For an MMD model, the reference text starts by importing PMX in Blender,
   rescaling the model, and checking global alignment against the original
   RE-game skeleton. Leg and foot alignment may need separate work. The
   reference text explicitly leaves
   some results unresolved, so this must not be presented as an automatic step.
2. Correct scale, pose, FBX materials, or missing-texture problems in Blender,
   then pass the FBX to 3ds Max. Mesh splitting can be done in Blender or Max;
   Skin Wrap, weight transfer, renaming, and export follow afterward.
3. Find the native mesh that should be replaced first, then give the custom
   mesh that native name. The reference text notes that a thigh or leg native
   mesh name is often important, but this is not a naming rule that applies to
   all models.
4. Its EM4700 example splits the custom model into head, upper body, and legs.
   For LOD 1, the recorded target slots are Head Mesh 62, Legs Mesh 37, and
   Upper Body Mesh 15. These numbers are an EM4700 sample, not fixed slots for
   player characters or other enemies.
5. The reference text calls Mesh 7 a mesh without vertices but with Skin
   modifiers and says to skip it. It also skipped Skin Wrap on Mesh 61, the tooth mesh,
   because of an older exporter issue. The second point is historical: test the
   current exporter instead of treating it as a permanent rule.
6. Finish one set of LOD meshes, then clone instances for other LODs rather
   than independently rebuilding every mesh at every LOD. The reference text
   suggests that LOD 2 groups drive in-game shadows. This document treats that only as
   an EM4700 observation, not as a rule for players or other resources.
7. If `RE6 COPY INSTANCE.ms` is used, it can batch-generate cloned instances
   for LOD levels. The reference text says it has no automatic recognition, so
   mesh entries still need to be added, processed, and cleared manually.

UV2 remapping and inspection:

Why the RE6 script needs UV2 export: I am not skilled at arranging UV1. In my
personal workflow, I import the model into 3ds Max, run Pack & Normalize on
UV2 so UV2 carries one complete, unfolded, non-overlapping texture layout, and
do not touch the original UV1. The RE6 script therefore needs to preserve and
export that UV2 result for the downstream workflow. Other MOD authors may be
more skilled and organize UVs in a better way. This is a personal workaround,
not a required or best-practice workflow for everyone.

1. The reference text suggests using 3DS as a side-by-side reference because
   its import and export state is closest to native .MOD.
2. Add an Unwrap UVW modifier to every mesh intended for export, then switch to
   Channel 2.
3. Use Move and Pack & Normalize to spread the UV islands completely with no
   overlaps.
4. Move Unwrap UVW below the Skin and Skin Wrap modifiers, then use Collapse To
   to bake the new UV2 layout into Editable Mesh.
5. Meshes on the same layer may be temporarily combined with Attach for unified
   inspection; restore the pre-merge state after inspection.

Texture baking:

1. Press `0` to open Render to Texture and select `Using Existing Channel = 2`.
2. Add DiffuseMap under Output and use the three-dot control to select PNG as
   the output format.
3. In Color Management, use `Color Space Conversion` with sRGB. The reference
   text warns that `No Conversion` produces dull, desaturated textures.
4. Set Target Map Slot to Base Color, Element Background to solid white, and
   resolution to 4096 x 4096. Enable `Render to Files Only` under Baked Material
   and bake. The resulting images are base assets for later Base, Normal, and
   Metallic-map work; they are not automatically correct final maps.
5. The reference text's older DDS note says to disable MIPMAP, use DXT5
   Interpolated Alpha for BM and NM, and use DXT1 1-bit Alpha for a black-and-
   white MM. That differs from this document's current personal settings in
   section 4 (DXT1 No Alpha for BM/MM and DXT5 Interpolated Alpha for NM). Do
   not mix the two blindly: choose the final format from the target TEX version,
   source texture contract, and read-back/in-game verification.

Skin seams between adjacent meshes:

The RE6 script provides weight-seam handling between different meshes. Current
results are not yet satisfactory and require further research and testing.

4. Current texture settings for playable characters: personal working values

The following settings are currently used in this workflow:

    Size: 4096 x 4096
    BM: DXT1, No Alpha
    MM: DXT1, No Alpha
    NM: DXT5 Interpolated Alpha
    MIPMAP: do not generate

They are not universal RE6 requirements. This workflow places every UV island
on one texture, while official resources often split head and body maps.
Putting a large unified map back into an ARC without changing texture paths can
make one ARC impractically large, potentially hundreds of GB.

The MRL is therefore redirected to loose custom texture files below the game's
nativePC directory. An MRL path must begin with the first directory below
nativePC:

    Correct: 1\2\3
    Incorrect: \1\2\3

The game resolves the file as:

    nativePC\1\2\3

Do not start the MRL string with a backslash and do not write the nativePC
prefix in it. See `AI MRL PROJECT\01_MRL_Texture_Path_Redirection_CN_EN.txt`
in this release tree for the complete rules and examples.

5. Missing resources and test switches

With Ultimate Trainer's Missing Files Fix enabled, removing a resource may not
crash the game immediately. That can help diagnose resource-chain and path
problems, but it does not mean the resulting MOD is stable enough to release.

It remains untested whether Wilsonso's shader pack always avoids Missing File
dialogs or crashes when a TEX resource inside an ARC has been removed. Treat
this as future homework: test it and record the result instead of treating it
as a verified conclusion.

6. EM4700 / EM4701 texture, hands, and UV observation

Enemy resources are usually concentrated in their relevant ARC set. "No
separate hand MOD" does not mean that hands do not need a MOD. It is a workflow
choice made to avoid separately creating and binding hands, and the real
resource chain still needs confirmation.

In the current EM4700 observation, the game reads EM4700's own hand UV rather
than the EM4701 pattern. The EM4700 / EM4701 body textures were also actually
read from the EM4700 set; editing the corresponding EM4701 texture assets had
no effect. One unverified hypothesis is that the EM4701 .MOD may also read
EM4700 UV. It needs binary read-back and in-game single-variable testing before
it can be treated as a conclusion.

If EM4701 can appear independently, the EM4700 and EM4701 UV islands need to
be compatible, preferably with the same layout. Otherwise EM4701's upper-body
texture can be scrambled.

Player-character hands also have a separate HAND ARC. In the body file, most
hand meshes in a player-character .MOD appear on LOD 3. In the current test,
setting those mesh LOD fields to 0 made the character lose its hands in game.
The hand UV and HAND ARC UV appear to follow the UV of the modified mesh: when
the modified mesh already includes both hands, it can look normal in game
without an additional hand-weight bind. The reason remains unknown. This is a
labor-saving route used by many authors, not an explained or proven resource
contract.

Some player assets may appear to work with textures omitted while Missing Files
Fix is enabled, but that is a temporary test method, not a stable release
baseline. Future enemy, weapon, player, stage, and prop tests should record
which resource the game actually reads, rather than guessing only from
filenames.

7. Building a body, hands, and head

A common route is to complete a body that includes hands, attach and bind a
head, then smooth the weights in 3ds Max with weight tools and brushes. After
the work is complete and backed up, an original head mesh can be removed from a
body MOD to create a new MOD. Facial animation will usually still require work
on the head MOD or head-related resources.

8. Cross-ARC MOD / MRL substitution: an unverified hypothesis

With Wilsonso's shader pack present, or with Ultimate Trainer's Missing Files
Fix and Render Everything both enabled, it may be possible in some stage-ARC
and actor-ARC cases to place prepared .MOD and .MRL files across containers.
The attraction is fast reuse of a custom model while the target supplies its
own animation, sounds, and dialogue.

This is not a generally verified method. Texture-path collisions, animation
containers, bone counts, BMT, collision, and initialization resources all
create restrictions. No universal rule for all characters and enemies has been
established.

uSm5919 is a counterexample. uEm4700 MOD, MRL, and TEX files were placed in a
uSm5919 container. Removing an incompatible BMT made the full mesh visible,
but uEm4700 has 83 bones while the native uSm5919 animation container has only
27. The mesh still flickered or disappeared during animation. This proves that
display resources entered the container; it does not prove a stable character
replacement. The correct route is still to use the target's native skeleton as
the 3D-software template, rebind the mesh, and inspect its skin weights.

9. Why keep these notes

If future AI can genuinely understand 3D graphics, 3D software, and the whole
RE6 MOD workflow, people may be able to supply creative direction without
mastering every technical step alone. Farther in the future, after copyright
terms have expired and lawful rights exist, a deep understanding of game
resources may inspire a modern-engine reinterpretation for later generations.
That is a vision, not a current capability or permission to use protected work.

10. Final note

This is not a qualified complete MOD tutorial. The author does not currently
have the time, environment, or professional knowledge to cover every model,
weight, material, animation, and game-resource condition. Think with AI, keep
backups, read files back, and test in game. Respect other people's rights and
interests; focus on technical research and your own creation.
